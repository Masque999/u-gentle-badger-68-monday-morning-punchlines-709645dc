# Monday Morning Punchlines

One joke about Monday mornings, at most 200 characters.

Entry: `Monday morning: my alarm went off, my coffee filed a complaint, and my calendar already had three meetings titled 'quick sync' before I had even opened my eyes.`

Run: `python3 main.py` (writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt`).
